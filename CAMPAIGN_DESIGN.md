# Campaign set pieces

The player has two equally viable routes through the four stages. Free guidance has the strongest clear speed and earns a short kill-chain score bonus. Holding the orb prevents damage from enemy contact and deletes bullets only where the visible orb touches them; it deals no damage. Release returns the player to the offensive route. No encounter requires a single mandatory input.

| Module | Readable setup | Efficient response | Alternative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SPEAR LINE | Two slow, close-packed vertical columns | Move away, let a charged orb overrun toward the ship, then dodge so it penetrates a whole column | Throw along the column |
| SWEEP LINE | Two broad horizontal ranks, later curved | Swing the free orb across the width of the fleet | Pick off stragglers with release throws |
| MATADOR | Divers visibly lock the player's position before committing | Bait the aim, step sideways, and let the following orb spear their path | Capture to repel a diver, then release |
| PRESS & SPEAR | Two heavy shields screen pairs of light ships | Capture to push a side open or bunch enemies, then release into the exposed line | Circle wide and attack the outer edge |
| BULLET CURTAIN | A centered barrage bomber with offset snipers | Capture through the dense interval, move to a thinner side, and resume guidance | Stay in a clear lane and attack continuously |
| Stage 2 wall | Thick front armor, vulnerable rear and three reflectors | Enter from the side and use the rear pocket for repeated hits | Wear down the front with multiple precise throws |

Stage 1 introduces each module separately, with a large invader bank extending the horizontal sweep. Stage 2 combines the push setup with the reflector pocket. Stage 3 adds side swoops and a heavy cruiser alongside familiar shapes. Stage 4 layers divers with bullets, then asks for push, sweep, and vertical penetration in succession. Each wave has a gap before the next arrival, and bosses follow a warning after the final set piece.

On a free-orb kill, another kill within 90 frames grows a chain up to a 2× score multiplier. This rewards skillful penetration without changing damage or making capture ineffective as defense. A small `CHAIN` flash and brief screen shake mark every third kill. Formation wipes still grant their existing shield recovery, so newer players can recover without maintaining a chain.
